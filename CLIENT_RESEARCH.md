## Website Publication Guidance

- Use client names only when the source is a public Limitless post or an official public MSU/company source.
- Phrase work as “Town Hall,” “featured startup,” “discussion,” “student perspective,” or “strategic feedback themes” unless a formal consulting deliverable is approved.
- Do not publish metrics, revenue impact, market conclusions, decks, models, or recommendations without written client approval.
- If confidence is medium or low, keep the public copy broad and add details later after review.
