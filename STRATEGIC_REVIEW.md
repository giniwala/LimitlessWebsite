# Strategic Review

Date: April 30, 2026

This review is intentionally critical. The site looks professional, but a strong consulting organization site needs to answer different questions for different audiences quickly and honestly.

## Prospective Student Members

Update after this pass: The site now explains that students can start by attending open Townhalls, then move into deeper consulting work through recruiting, Workshops, and project teams. This reduces the biggest "what do I actually do here?" gap for applicants.

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | What Limitless is, who joins, what work feels like, how competitive recruiting is, what skills they build, and when to apply. |
| How well the site serves them | Good foundation. The Join page explains traits, process, and member development clearly. Team page makes the org feel real. |
| Friction remaining | Application link, recruiting dates, time commitment, interview format, and sample member outcomes are still placeholders or broad. |
| What would help | Add semester-specific recruiting timeline, “day in the life” member blurbs, sample training calendar, and alumni/internship outcomes once verified. |
| CTA clarity | “Apply Now” is clear, but it needs the real form and deadline context. |
| Trust signals | Real member photos, LinkedIn links, alumni roles, workshop photos, and faculty/advisor support would matter. |

## Current Members

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | Quick access to files, schedule, announcements, templates, and project links. |
| How well the site serves them | The password portal is simple and useful for a student org. |
| Friction remaining | Resources are still mostly placeholder links. Shared-password access cannot personalize content or revoke one member independently. |
| What would help | Add real Drive links, meeting decks, project timelines, member handbook, semester calendar, and project-team tabs. |
| CTA clarity | “Member Portal” is clear and consistent. |
| Trust signals | Members mainly need reliability, not marketing polish. Keep portal fast and boring. |

## Student Startup Clients

Update after this pass: The homepage and Services page now make the founder journey clearer: pitch at Townhall, share 1-3 challenges, get live student brainstorming, and continue through Thursday Workshops when there is fit.

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | What problems Limitless can help with, expected process, time commitment, confidentiality, and how to request support. |
| How well the site serves them | Services are strong and startup-focused. Client showcase adds credibility without exposing private work. |
| Friction remaining | No clear founder intake form, eligibility criteria, semester availability, or examples of approved deliverables. |
| What would help | Add a short “Work With Us” intake flow with project fit questions, expected timeline, confidentiality note, and sample approved deliverables. |
| CTA clarity | “Work With Us” is visible but should eventually lead to a form rather than only contact. |
| Trust signals | Public client names help. Approved founder quotes or faculty partner quotes would help more. |

## Professors / Faculty Advisors

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | Is the organization serious, safe for students, aligned with MSU entrepreneurship, and worth referring founders to? |
| How well the site serves them | Professional tone and conservative claims help credibility. |
| Friction remaining | The site does not yet name advisors, operating governance, faculty relationships, or referral process. |
| What would help | Add an advisor/partners section, faculty referral email, and one-page overview for referring student founders. |
| CTA clarity | Contact page works, but faculty-specific path is missing. |
| Trust signals | Advisor names, MSU partner references, and approved event photos would matter. |

## University Partners

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | Clear role in the entrepreneurship ecosystem, professionalism, student development value, and partnership opportunities. |
| How well the site serves them | About/services copy positions Limitless well within MSU’s entrepreneurship ecosystem. |
| Friction remaining | No explicit partnership page, metrics, academic-year impact summary, or collaboration model. |
| What would help | Add annual impact snapshots, event collaboration options, and a partner inquiry CTA. |
| CTA clarity | General contact is adequate but not tailored. |
| Trust signals | Verified stats, public event recaps, and university partner logos with permission. |

## Alumni

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | How to mentor, speak, donate, refer startups, or recruit members. |
| How well the site serves them | Current site has little alumni-specific content. |
| Friction remaining | No alumni path, speaker interest form, mentor roster, or donation/volunteer information. |

## General Visitors

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| What they want | Who Limitless is and whether it is legitimate. |
| How well the site serves them | Homepage now communicates brand, mission, clients, team, services, social links, and contact clearly. |
| Friction remaining | Placeholder stats and missing real event photos reduce trust. |
| What would help | Replace placeholders, add recent updates, and add authentic group photos. |
| CTA clarity | Good. Apply, Work With Us, and Member Portal are distinct. |
| Trust signals | Official social links, public client highlights, and real team headshots are strong. |

## Sharpest Strategic Recommendations

| Priority | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| High | Replace placeholders before launch: stats, application link, recruiting dates, and portal resource links. |
| High | Build a founder inquiry form with project-fit questions and expectation setting. |
| High | Add a short public-safe scope note for potential clients before collecting sensitive information. |
| Medium | Keep refreshing real group/event photos and add approved founder/member quotes when available. |
| Medium | Add an alumni/faculty partner pathway. |
| Medium | Add a curated updates/news section so the site feels alive between recruiting cycles. |
| Low | Add more brand motion only after content gaps are resolved. |
